# 1. Blockchain Technology: A Comprehensive Guide
A detailed reference guide covering the complete landscape of blockchain technology — from core concepts to real-world industry applications.

**Topics Covered:**
- Introduction to Blockchain & its key characteristics (Decentralization, Immutability, Transparency)
- How Blockchain works — Block Structure, Consensus Mechanisms (PoW, PoS, DPoS, PoA, PoH)
- Types of Blockchains — Public, Private, Consortium, Hybrid
- Use Cases across industries: Finance, Supply Chain, Healthcare, Government, Real Estate, Energy, NFTs, Legal
- Challenges & Limitations — Scalability, Energy, Regulation, Interoperability
- Future of Blockchain — Web3, CBDCs, AI Integration, Quantum-Resistant Cryptography
